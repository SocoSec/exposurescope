  The mean is 17x the median, so the distribution is dominated by a
  small number of very large corpora. Reporting an average breach size
  would therefore describe almost none of them.

THE CLASSES THIS MODEL WEIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  credential-bearing (Passwords)       64.3%
  permanent identity (Dates of birth)  27.5%
  contact (Phone numbers)              36.5%
  lookup key (Email addresses)         99.3%

  Passwords appear in 64.3% of catalogued breaches. Industry telemetry
  reports roughly 28%. The two populations differ: incidents reported by
  firms, against corpora collected and published. A tool scoring an
  individual against corpora should use the corpus figure.
